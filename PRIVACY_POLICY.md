# Privacy Policy for SmoothCalendar-NG

Last updated: October 20, 2026

## 1. Overview
SmoothCalendar-NG is an open source Android widget designed to display upcoming calendar events directly on your home screen. Your privacy is paramount. SmoothCalendar-NG operates fully offline and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

## 2. Permissions and Data Access
The application requests the following Android runtime permission:

* **READ_CALENDAR (`android.permission.READ_CALENDAR`):**
  * **Purpose:** Required strictly to query your local device calendars and display upcoming events inside the home screen widget and configuration preview.
  * **Processing:** Calendar data is processed entirely in memory on your device.
  * **Storage & Transmission:** Calendar events are neither saved to persistent files nor transmitted outside the app. The app does not request or possess the Android `INTERNET` permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`). Therefore, data transmission over the network is technically impossible.

## 3. Data Collection and Sharing
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
* **Analytics & Tracking:** SmoothCalendar-NG contains no third-party SDKs, tracking libraries, advertising networks, or analytics tools.
* **Third-Party Disclosure:** Because no data is collected, no data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
SmoothCalendar-NG does not target children under the age of 13 and collects no personal information whatsoever.
